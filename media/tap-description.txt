The tap is chrome, or something that looks like chrome.
It has a single lever handle on top. When you lift it,
water comes out.
When you push it down, water stops.
The spout is curved, like a neck bent slightly forward.
There is a small aerator at the end, 
and if you look closely it is furred with white scale.
The base has a ring of black rubber where it meets the sink. 
It does not look old or new.
It looks like every tap you have ever used and never looked at.
I have looked at it twenty times now and I still do not know if I have seen it.
